description=Interactive ER Mapper-style colour stretch for single-band rasters
about=Edit a colour stretch curve directly over the data histogram, with live updates on the map. Linear, histogram-equalised and log-linear presets; percentile or value clip limits; a symmetric mirror mode for grids that straddle zero (e.g. magnetic 1VD); undo/redo. Export georeferenced images (Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFF, GeoTIFF, JPEG2000, PNG, JPEG) with transparent collars and any cell size, plus matching colour bars (PNG, SVG, PDF). Built for geophysics grids such as magnetics, gravity and radiometrics. No extra dependencies: it uses NumPy and GDAL, which come with QGIS, and works on Windows, macOS and Linux. Includes a small synthetic sample dataset (Raster > Stretch Editor > Load sample data).
