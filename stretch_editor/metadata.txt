Export georeferenced images (Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFF, GeoTIFF, JPEG2000, PNG, JPEG) with transparent collars and any cell size, plus matching colour bars (PNG, SVG, PDF). Built for geophysics grids such as magnetics, gravity and radiometrics.
