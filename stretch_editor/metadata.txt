description=SDP Imaging (SDP = Spatial Data Portal): interactive ER Mapper-style colour stretching for single-band geophysics grids
about=Formerly Stretch Editor. Edit a colour stretch curve directly over the data histogram, with live updates on the map. Linear, histogram-equalised and log-linear presets; percentile or value clip limits; a symmetric mirror mode for grids that straddle zero (e.g. magnetic 1VD); undo/redo. Export georeferenced images (Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFF, GeoTIFF, JPEG2000, PNG, JPEG) with transparent collars and any cell size, plus matching colour bars (PNG, SVG, PDF). Ternary (RGB) images from three grids, e.g. radiometric K, Th and U, with a separate curve, presets and clip limits per channel, CMY inversion and a triangle legend. Built for geophysics grids such as magnetics, gravity and radiometrics. No extra dependencies: it uses NumPy and GDAL, which come with QGIS, and works on Windows, macOS and Linux. Includes a small synthetic sample dataset (Raster > SDP Imaging > Load sample data).
